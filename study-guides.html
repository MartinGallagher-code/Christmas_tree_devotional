Family Resources

Study Guides

A printable study guide for each week of Advent. Every guide divides the week's readings into four sessions — one for the Sunday gathering when the tree changes, and three short weekday sittings that can be combined if a day is missed. Reflection questions sit at the points where the readings open something worth lingering on.

Week One Study Guide

The Tree

The two trees of Eden, the tree as a picture of the faithful life, the tree of the curse becoming the tree of the cross, and the Tree of Life restored.

Week Two Study Guide

The Light

Light before all else, the honest darkness of Job and the psalms, the prophets' promise of dawn, and the city where God Himself is the light forever.

Week Three Study Guide

Prophecy & Heritage

The command to remember, the prophetic promise narrowing across centuries, the day Jesus stood up and said "today this is fulfilled," and the heritage that endures.

Week Four Study Guide

The Gift & the Prize

The gift promised in Eden, anticipated through psalms and prophets, arriving in a manger, revealed as God Himself — and the question every person must answer. The final block is written for Christmas morning.

How These Are Meant to Be Used

Each guide is a four-session walk through the week's Scripture readings. The readings are grouped thematically inside each session, with reflection questions placed at the natural break-points — not after every verse. Read the passage aloud, then sit with one question at a time. The aim is conversation, not completion.

Each guide includes a Print this guide button at the top, and the print stylesheet starts each session on its own page so a printed copy is easy to share around a table.
